Platform Listing Agreement (PLA)

Version 1.4 Ref: SKC-PLA-2025-v1.4 Effective: 1 January 2025 ISO 9001:2015 Aligned
ℹ️ This agreement applies specifically to Service Providers who list services on SkillConnect. Acceptance is confirmed by checking the PLA checkbox on the "Post a Service" form.

1. Provider Eligibility

Any individual aged 18 or above residing in Pakistan may list a service on SkillConnect. Regulated services (medical, legal, financial, electrical, structural engineering) require the provider to hold valid professional licences.

2. Listing Accuracy

Providers warrant that all listing information — title, description, pricing, qualifications, contact details — is accurate, truthful, and not misleading. Providers must promptly update listings if information changes. Misrepresentation may result in immediate removal and legal consequences.

3. CPID Assignment & Responsibility

Upon submission, each listing is assigned a unique CPID (SKC-XXXX). This identifier is permanent and must not be falsified. Providers are responsible for all activity associated with their CPID. CPIDs are used for accountability, dispute resolution, and ISO 9001 quality tracking.

4. Prohibited Listings

  1. Illegal or unlicensed professional services
  2. Deceptive, fraudulent, or misleading offers
  3. Content infringing third-party intellectual property
  4. Services promoting harm, hate, or discrimination
  5. Spam, duplicate, or automated listings

5. Pricing & Payments

SkillConnect does not process or facilitate payments. All pricing shown is indicative only and negotiated directly between provider and client. Providers are solely responsible for applicable taxes on income earned through services found via the Platform.

6. Quality Commitment

Providers commit to performing services with reasonable care and skill, consistent with ISO 9001:2015 quality management principles. Providers agree to respond to feedback professionally and resolve complaints in good faith.

7. Data & Visibility

By listing, providers consent to public display of their name, contact information, category, and service description. Data is processed per our Privacy Policy and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 controls. Providers may request removal at any time.

8. Termination

SkillConnect may remove any listing at its discretion for PLA violations without prior notice. Providers may remove their listings by contacting support@skillconnect.pk referencing their CPID.

9. Indemnification

Providers agree to indemnify and hold SkillConnect harmless from any claims, damages, losses, or expenses (including legal fees) arising from: (a) their service provision; (b) listing content; (c) violation of this PLA or any applicable law.

Consumer Product Identification (CPID) Framework

Standard: SKC-CPID-v1.0 Effective: 1 January 2025 ISO 9001:2015 Aligned
ℹ️ Every service listing on SkillConnect is assigned a unique CPID (Consumer Product Identification code) upon submission. This code is your permanent listing reference.

1. What is a CPID?

A CPID (Consumer Product Identification code) is a unique alphanumeric identifier assigned to each service listing on SkillConnect. Format: SKC-XXXX (e.g., SKC-1001, SKC-1002). CPIDs are inspired by standard product identification frameworks used in consumer protection, supply chain management, and digital commerce.

2. Purpose of CPID

  1. Traceability: Each listing can be uniquely referenced in support queries, disputes, or reports.
  2. Accountability: Providers are permanently linked to their CPID, discouraging fraudulent or misleading listings.
  3. Quality Tracking: CPIDs enable the platform to track ratings and feedback per listing, consistent with ISO 9001:2015 quality management.
  4. Dispute Resolution: Any complaint, report, or support request should reference the relevant CPID for efficient processing.

3. CPID Assignment Process

  1. Provider submits a listing via the "Post a Service" form.
  2. Provider accepts the Platform Listing Agreement (PLA v1.4).
  3. Upon submission, the system assigns the next available CPID in the SKC-XXXX series.
  4. CPID is displayed on the listing card, in the contact modal, and in the provider's submission confirmation.
  5. The CPID is permanent — it does not change even if listing details are updated.

4. CPID Rules for Providers

  1. Providers must not falsify, duplicate, or transfer their CPID to another listing or provider.
  2. Providers must include their CPID in any support, complaint, or removal request.
  3. Misuse of CPID constitutes a violation of the PLA and may result in listing removal.

5. CPID Rules for Users

  1. Users should note the CPID of any listing they engage with.
  2. Users must reference the CPID when submitting a complaint or report about a listing.
  3. Users must not misrepresent a CPID or use it to create false disputes.
